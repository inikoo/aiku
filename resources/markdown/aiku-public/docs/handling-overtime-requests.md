<aside class="tldr">
<b>HR → Overtime</b> lists every overtime request as a card. Approve or reject the pending ones; once a request has a decision it can't be approved, rejected or edited again. HR can also enter a request for someone with <b>New overtime request</b>. The <b>Dashboard</b> tab shows the month, and <b>Overtime types</b> holds the kinds of overtime people can choose from.
</aside>

## Asking for overtime (employee)

On your **Employee Clocking** page, open the **Overtime** tab and press **New overtime request**. Choose the **Overtime type** and the **Date worked**, give the **Start time** and the **Duration** in hours and minutes, and say why under **Reason**. **Submit Request** sends it to HR. While it is pending you can still change it.

## Finding a request (HR)

Open **HR → Overtime**. Each card shows the staff member, the overtime type, what was **Requested** (the date, the start time and how long), what was **Recorded**, the reason, and the **Decision** with who made it and when.

Above the cards you can search by staff member, approver, type or reason, pick a status and set a **From** and **To** date. **Clear filters** resets them. **Export** downloads the overtime report as Excel or CSV.

## Approving and rejecting

On a pending card:

- **Approve**, then **Yes, approve**.
- **Reject**, then **Yes, reject**.
- **Edit** to correct the request before deciding it.

After the decision the card shows **Processed**. A decided request can't be approved, rejected or edited again.

**Delete** is there for any request. If it has already been processed, aiku says so before you confirm.

## Entering overtime for someone

Press **New overtime request** at the top of the page.

1. Pick the **Staff member**, the **Overtime type** and the **Date worked**.
2. Give the start time and the duration that were asked for.
3. Under **Recorded time**, leave **Same as requested**, or choose **Different time** and enter the **Recorded start time** and **Recorded duration** that were really worked.
4. Choose the **Status** and add a **Reason** if you want one.
5. Press **Submit request**.

When the recorded time differs from the requested one, the card shows the difference next to **Recorded**.

## Overtime types

The **Overtime types** tab is the list people choose from. **New overtime type** adds one, with a **Code**, a **Name**, an optional **Color** and **Description**, and:

- **Category**: Overtime, Flexi time, Working time or Other.
- **Compensation type**: Paid, Time in lieu or Unpaid.
- **Multiplier**, if this type is paid at a rate.

The list marks each type **Active** or **Inactive**, and each one can be edited or deleted.

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab is the month at a glance: one row per employee, one column per day. Filter by employee or overtime type, step through the months, and click an entry to see its **Overtime Details**.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li>Asking for overtime needs an employee record linked to your aiku login.</li>
<li>Seeing the requests needs <b>HR view</b> rights in the organisation. A section supervisor sees the requests of their own section only, and can't change them.</li>
<li>Approving, rejecting, editing, deleting and entering requests need <b>HR edit</b> rights. So does changing the overtime types.</li>
</ul>
