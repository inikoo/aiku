<aside class="tldr">
<b>HR → Bulk emails</b> is for the message everyone needs: a closure day, a new rule, the payslip date. Press <b>New bulk email</b>, write it, pick who gets it on the right, and send. Nobody is selected to begin with, so an email never goes to the whole company by accident. Every email you send stays in the list, and opening one shows exactly what was sent and to whom.
</aside>

## Writing the email

Open **HR → Bulk emails** and press **New bulk email**. The email is written on its own page.

On the left:

- **Subject**.
- **Message**. The editor has headings, bold, lists, colours and links.
- **Attachments**: up to 5 files of 5 MB each.

On the right, the **Recipients**:

- The list is your working employees. It starts with **Nobody selected yet**.
- The dropdown switches between **All working employees** and **Selected employees**. Choosing all ticks everyone who can be reached.
- Search by name or job title, then tick people one by one, or press **Select all**. While you are searching, the button reads **Select shown** and ticks only the people in view.
- **Clear** unticks everyone.
- Someone marked **no email** has no address on their employee record and can't be ticked.

The count at the top of the panel tells you how many are selected out of how many can be reached.

## Sending

The send button shows how many people will get the email. It works once there is a subject, a message and at least one recipient. aiku asks you to confirm, because emails go out straight away and can't be recalled.

Each person gets the email at the work email on their employee record, or at their personal email if they have no work one. To add a missing address, see <a href="/docs/setting-up-a-new-employee">Setting up a new employee</a>.

## Emails already sent

**HR → Bulk emails** lists what has been sent, newest first. Each card shows the subject, the start of the message, how many employees received it, the attachments, who sent it and when.

Search by subject or message, and narrow the list with **From** and **To**. **Clear filters** resets them.

Click a card to open the email:

- **From** and **Sent**, then the message as it went out.
- **Attachments**, each with a download. A file that is gone shows **File no longer available**.
- **Recipients**: everyone it was sent to, with a search. Someone who has since left is marked **no longer working**.

Emails sent before recipients were kept show the number of recipients only, with the note that the list was not recorded.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>Write an email:</b> your organisation → <b>HR → Bulk emails</b> → <b>New bulk email</b>.</li>
<li><b>Send to everyone:</b> in <b>Recipients</b>, choose <b>All working employees</b>.</li>
<li><b>Check who received one:</b> <b>HR → Bulk emails</b> → click the email → <b>Recipients</b>.</li>
<li><b>Get an attachment back:</b> the download on the email's card, or on the email's own page.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li>Reading sent emails and downloading their attachments needs <b>HR view</b> rights in the organisation.</li>
<li>Writing and sending a bulk email needs <b>HR edit</b> rights.</li>
</ul>
</aside>
