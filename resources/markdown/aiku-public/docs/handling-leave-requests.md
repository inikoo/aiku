<aside class="tldr">
<b>HR → Leave Requests</b> lists every leave request as a card. Approvers decide pending requests there, and HR can record leave for someone, which is approved as it is saved. A request that has been approved or rejected can't be decided again. The <b>Dashboard</b> tab is the leave calendar, and each employee's page has a <b>Leave</b> tab with their own history.
</aside>

Staff ask for leave themselves, from the **Leave** tab of their **Employee Clocking** page, with **Request Leave**. What they send arrives here as *pending*.

## Finding a request

Open **HR → Leave Requests**. Each card shows the employee, the leave type, the **Leave period** with the number of days, how far the **Approval** has got, the reason, any attachments and who the leave is **Covered by**.

Above the cards:

- **Search** by employee, leave type or reason.
- **Status**: **All**, **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**.
- **All leave types** narrows the list to one type.
- **From** and **To** show the leave that falls inside those dates.
- **Clear filters** puts everything back.

**Export** downloads the leave report as Excel or CSV, for the dates, type, status, department and employee you choose.

## Recording leave for someone

Press **Record leave** when the request did not come through aiku: a sick day phoned in, a holiday agreed on paper.

1. Pick the **Employee**.
2. Pick the **Leave Type**, the **Start Date** and the **End Date**.
3. Optionally choose who it is **Covered by**. See <a href="/docs/covering-for-a-colleague-on-leave">Covering for a colleague on leave</a>.
4. Add a **Reason** if you want one on record.
5. Press **Record leave**.

Leave recorded this way is approved immediately. It does not wait for an approver.

## Approving and rejecting

A pending card shows **Approve** and **Reject** to the people who are approvers for that request. Approvers are set on the **Leave Approval** tab of the same page.

- **Approve** opens **Approve leave**, where you can also choose the cover, then **Yes, approve**.
- **Reject** asks for a **Rejection Reason**. The employee sees it, and it stays on the card.

Where a request needs more than one approver, the **Approval** box shows the level reached, for example *Level 1 of 2*, and who has already decided. The leave is approved when the last level approves it. A rejection at any level rejects it.

Once a request is approved or rejected it shows **Processed** and the buttons go. It can't be approved or rejected a second time.

## Editing

**Edit** is there for approvers, on pending requests only. You can change the leave type, the dates, the reason, the cover and the attachments. New attachments replace the ones already there.

## Deleting

**Delete** removes a request for good, so aiku asks first and tells you what you are about to undo:

- **Pending**: the request has not been decided yet, and the approvers will no longer see it.
- **Approved**: the leave comes off the employee's leave record and the leave calendar, and any absence cover assigned to it ends. The button reads **Delete anyway**.
- **Rejected**: the request and the reason it was rejected come off the employee's leave record. The button reads **Delete anyway**.

## The leave calendar

The **Dashboard** tab is the calendar: one row per employee, one column per day, each leave drawn in the colour of its type. Leave still waiting for approval is striped, and holidays and weekends are shaded.

- Switch between **Month** and **Week**, and step back and forward with the arrows.
- Search for a name, or filter by employee, leave type or department.
- **Sort by** name, last name, first name or department.
- Click a leave to see its **Leave Details**.
- **Export** downloads the leave report as Excel or CSV, or opens it as a **Print View**.

The **Leave calendar** quick action on the <a href="/docs/reading-the-hr-dashboard">HR dashboard</a> opens this same page.

## One employee's leave

Open an employee and choose the **Leave** tab.

- **Period** picks a year, or **All time**. It starts on the current year.
- The tiles count **Total requests**, **Approved**, **Pending** and **Rejected**, plus the **Days taken**. Click a tile to list only those requests.
- **Leave balance** and **By leave type** show how the approved days are split.
- **Leave requests** lists each request with the same details as the cards on the main page.
- **Open in Leave Requests** takes you to the main page, already searched for this employee, where you can act on a request.

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Where to click in aiku</strong>
<ul>
<li><b>All requests:</b> your organisation → <b>HR → Leave Requests</b>.</li>
<li><b>What is waiting:</b> set <b>Status</b> to <b>Pending</b>.</li>
<li><b>Enter leave for someone:</b> <b>Record leave</b>, on Leave Requests or from the HR dashboard's quick actions.</li>
<li><b>Who is off, and when:</b> <b>HR → Leave Requests</b> → <b>Dashboard</b>.</li>
<li><b>One person's history:</b> <b>HR → Employees</b> → open the employee → <b>Leave</b>.</li>
<li><b>Who covers an absence:</b> <b>HR → Absence cover</b>.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<aside class="wayfinder"><strong>Permissions you need</strong>
<ul>
<li>Opening Leave Requests needs <b>HR view</b> rights in the organisation, or being an active leave approver. A section supervisor sees the requests of their own section only.</li>
<li><b>Approve</b> and <b>Reject</b> are for the approvers of that request. <b>Edit</b> is for active leave approvers.</li>
<li><b>Record leave</b> and <b>Delete</b> need <b>HR edit</b> rights.</li>
<li>The leave calendar needs <b>HR view</b> rights.</li>
</ul>
</aside>
