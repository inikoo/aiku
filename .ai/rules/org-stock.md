# Org Stock

## The unified label is the same in every organisation and never prints with a gap
HELP-3937 (13 Oct 2026). PdfOrgStockLabel with standard=unified delegates to PdfOrgStockUnifiedLabel: unit 100x60 (EAN-13), SKO 100x50 and carton 100x70 (GS1-128, AI 01 GTIN-14 + AI 10 batch). Nothing on it depends on the printing organisation: operators are every organisation's settings.gpsr.operator (see the GPSR rule below), warnings are gathered from the products of every organisation. A missing required value goes in blockers and the PDF is refused (422), never printed with a hole; a new required field means a new blocker, not a tick box. GTIN-14 is derived, not stored: indicator 1 SKO / 2 carton, 3-8 for further stocks sharing the unit EAN in stock id order; stocks.barcode and carton_barcode are untouched, so old labels keep scanning. ScanSkoBarcode reads the GS1-128 through parseGs1. The localised labels (tick boxes, Aurora sizes) stay as the exception.

## GPSR responsible persons and importers are organisation settings, never constants
23 Oct 2026. Each importing organisation (aw, es, sk) holds its legal identity in organisations.settings.gpsr.operator {code, position, role, short, name, address, email}: the registered address, not the warehouse address. groups.settings.gpsr.{eu,uk}_responsible_organisation_id says which organisation is the EU / UK Responsible Person; an organisation may override with the same keys in its own settings. Read through Organisation::getGpsrResponsiblePerson('eu'|'uk') and format with Organisation::formatGpsrOperator. A trade unit's gpsr_eu_responsible text overrides (getEffectiveGpsrEuResponsible); a product takes the override only from its single trade unit, never from products.gpsr_eu_responsible, which holds junk and stale copies. Only group admins (sysadmin.edit) see and save the GPSR section of the organisation settings, since it changes what every organisation prints. Labels, product pages, compliance pages and ProductLookupTool all read from here; never hardcode a name, address or email. The EU RP for every organisation, unless the trade unit's gpsr_eu_responsible is filled, is Ancient Wisdom s.r.o., Prílohy 583/57, 919 26 Zavar, Slovensko, contact@awgifts.eu. Never info@awgifts.eu: that was the stale value of the old GetOrgStockUnifiedLabelData::OPERATORS constant, which disagreed with GetOrgStockLabelData::EU_RESPONSIBLE_PERSON until both were replaced by these settings.
